What is a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

A LoveGoBuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping research. A useful entry identifies the product and its source, then leaves room to compare the exact option, size, current price and available inspection photos.

How to use this LoveGoBuy spreadsheet

Identify the item

Save its item reference and original marketplace URL. Similar photos may belong to different sellers or variants.

Compare the option

Record size, color, quantity and measurements. Compare garment measurements against something you already own.

Review the evidence

Inspect available listing details and any relevant QC photos. Ask for missing measurements before approving shipment.

Confirm the total

Check purchasing eligibility, seller terms and current charges on the platform handling your order.

Sizing & quality checks

A better shortlist starts with evidence.

  • Clothing: compare chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length and garment length in the same units.
  • Footwear: distinguish foot length from insole length; ask how the seller measured the size chart.
  • QC photos: check the actual option, labels, visible damage, seams, pair consistency and requested measurements.
  • Missing evidence: record it as unknown. Product photos alone do not prove authenticity or quality.

LoveGoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the products on this page come from?

The six entries were selected from the Hipobuyfind homepage. Their source item IDs and product links are preserved. The selection is a static snapshot rather than a complete or continuously synchronized catalog.

Do the main buttons take me to LoveGoBuy?

No. “LoveGoBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” both open https://hipobuyfind.com. Product references open individual Hipobuyfind pages. Check the destination and its role before sharing account or payment information.

Can I order every listed item through LoveGoBuy?

Compatibility has not been verified. Obtain the original marketplace link and confirm whether your chosen platform accepts that specific item, seller and category.

Are product prices and stock live?

This page does not display live prices or stock. Open the source listing and confirm the chosen variant and all current charges before placing an order.

Does a source photo count as a QC photo?

A seller image illustrates a listing. A warehouse QC photo documents an inspected item. Check which kind of image you are viewing, which listing it belongs to and whether it shows your actual order.

How should I compare shipping costs?

Compare quotes using the same destination and parcel assumptions. Check chargeable weight, route restrictions, included services and any additional charges before deciding.

What if a product link stops working?

Use the saved item name and reference to look for a current listing in the source directory. Check seller and variant details again rather than assuming a visually similar replacement is identical.

Who handles returns and order questions?

Contact the seller or platform that processes your transaction. Review its current terms before payment and raise item problems while applicable return options remain available.
